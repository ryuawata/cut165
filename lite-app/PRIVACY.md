# CUT365 Lite Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

CUT365 Lite is an offline-first food logging application. As currently designed, the app does not require an account, does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and does not transmit your food entries, calorie or protein estimates, or personal goals to our servers. Food entries, saved foods, and goal settings are stored locally on your device.

You may edit and remove entries within the app. Uninstalling the application may permanently delete its locally stored data. There is no cloud backup or cross-device synchronization in this version.

Apple processes purchases and related transactions when you buy the app through the App Store according to Apple's own policies. We do not receive your stored meal log as part of that transaction.

The app is not a medical device and does not provide medical or nutritional advice. Values entered by users are not independently validated for nutritional accuracy.

For privacy or support inquiries, use the official support contact published on the App Store listing.

This policy must be hosted at a publicly accessible URL and the developer must add a working contact method before App Store submission. Reassess it if services or analytics are added.
